## Which model a reader needs

- The **v5** model (`v5/`, `paper/RCR/figure_build/`) produced the current manuscript's results.
- The **v4** model (`models/v4/`, `scripts/v4/`, `scripts/advanced/`) is the earlier implementation, kept so that the analysis that preceded the current manuscript remains inspectable. Its state as first published is reachable at commit `9f104a244c844d21d73876c9d28fdb75f8322c63`.
